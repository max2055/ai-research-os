# Source

## Source summary

METR reports a randomized controlled trial of 16 experienced open-source developers and 246 tasks in which access to early-2025 AI tools increased completion time by 19%.

## Why it matters

Provides causal counterevidence to generalized productivity claims and contrasts measured outcomes with participant and expert forecasts.

## Reliability notes

Primary research paper with randomized task assignment. External validity is limited by the small cohort, mature familiar repositories and February–June 2025 tool frontier.
