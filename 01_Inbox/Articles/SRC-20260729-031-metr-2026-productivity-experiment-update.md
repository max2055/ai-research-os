# Source

## Source summary

METR says a later productivity experiment produced an unreliable current-effect signal because of participant and task selection, pay changes and concurrent-agent time measurement.

## Why it matters

Qualifies both the earlier slowdown result and later speedup narratives by documenting why current task-level productivity is difficult to identify.

## Relevant sections

- Experiment reliability assessment
- Raw estimates and confidence intervals
- Recruitment and task-selection effects

## Reliability notes

Primary methodological update that explicitly labels its evidence weak. Its beliefs about excluded developers are based partly on interviews and surveys rather than observed randomized outcomes.
