# AI Research OS v0.3 Known Limitations

Status: engineering baseline
Updated: 2026-08-11
Scope: F-020 product, data, model, operational, license, and decision boundaries

This document separates measured Facts, reasoned Inferences, and operator Judgments.
It does not convert implementation evidence into an investment conclusion or release
approval.

## Facts

### Data coverage and freshness

- The measured repository has 1034 formal objects, 156 Sources, 54 Events, 59
  Companies, 9 Sectors, 22 Impact Assertions, and 1490 Candidate rows at the F-012
  benchmark baseline. Source: `00_System/v0.3_Performance_Benchmark.md`.
- All 22 current Impact Assertions remain pending, so the reviewed direct-impact
  read model honestly returns zero reviewed direct assertions. No assertion was
  upgraded for Dashboard completeness.
- Channel/entity coverage and schedule freshness depend on reviewed Channel metadata,
  source links, and successful local jobs. Sparse `source_channel_ids` can produce an
  empty stale-company rollup even when other evidence exists.
- Candidate retention can remove dismissed/expired Candidate rows while preserving
  the append-only action audit. Candidate data is operational state, not the
  authoritative evidence archive.

### Model uncertainty and provider retention

- Analysis modes and runs preserve frozen inputs, model identifiers, parameters, and
  outputs, but many current runs use deterministic or echo adapters. Their existence
  does not establish external-model quality.
- Provider behavior, model versions, availability, privacy policy, and provider
  retention can change outside this repository. The system cannot independently
  prove how a provider retains prompts or outputs.
- Prompt injection text is escaped/rendered as data and security regression tests
  cover the current boundaries. This does not prove safety against every future
  parser, provider, or browser vulnerability.

### License and source access

- The 20 enabled Channels have reviewed repository license metadata. The audit is a
  metadata governance check, not fresh legal advice or live robots verification.
  Source: `00_System/v0.3_Channel_License_Audit.md`.
- Public availability does not imply unrestricted archiving, redistribution, or model
  training rights. Login, paywall, anti-bot bypass, and prohibited access are outside
  the capture workflow.
- Raw Source assets remain outside Git due to size, privacy, and license constraints.
  Eight current Source records intentionally have no archived asset.

### Storage, scale, and single-user operation

- The product is local-first and single-user. SQLite stores the Candidate operational
  queue; Markdown remains the research authority.
- The real operational baseline remains 1490 Candidate rows. A separate, disposable,
  schema-current 10,000-row fixture measured the full filter/order/duplicate-collapse/
  pagination/render path at p95 1.034069 seconds against the <2-second SLO. Source:
  `00_System/v0.3_Performance_Benchmark.md`.
- The 10,000-row result is local and single-user. It does not measure multi-user
  concurrency, distributed operation or scheduling, or 100,000-row Candidate scale.
  The 1501-object synthetic repository benchmark likewise is not a concurrency test.
- PostgreSQL, a graph database, a vector store, and a persistent cross-request cache
  are not enabled. Current request-scoped object preloading avoids stale authority.
- The durable backup service can snapshot Candidate SQLite and Source assets, encrypt
  both sets with `age`, upload immutable ciphertext/outer manifests to a fixed private
  GitHub prerelease, verify remote bytes, and restore to a disposable directory. This
  is an implemented capability, not evidence that an operator has configured or run a
  production remote backup.
- Backup repository, public recipients, private identities, off-device receipt custody,
  daily scheduling, and a second independent identity kept offline remain operator
  configuration. Current verify/restore commands require the ignored local receipt;
  losing every receipt is not recovered automatically from remote outer manifests.
- Health reads the local durable receipt without a remote request. Receipt freshness
  therefore proves the last create transaction completed, not that GitHub is reachable
  now; `verify-remote` is a separate explicit operation.

### Cost completeness

- Monthly cost monitoring derives a single-currency natural month total from known
  `discovery_runs.cost_estimate` values and the ignored
  `RESEARCH_OS_MODEL_COST_BUDGET` setting.
- Its six states are `unconfigured`, `no_data`, `ok`, `warning`, `exceeded`, and
  `invalid`. The operating rule "missing cost is not zero" is enforced by reporting
  unknown and invalid records separately.
- A known-cost total is only the sum of recorded estimates. It is not independently
  reconciled to provider invoices and cannot prove complete provider spend.

### Web and automation boundary

- The read-only Web UI binds to loopback and has no authoritative research mutation.
  The only non-GET routes are `/llm/config`, `/llm/models`, and `/llm/test` for local
  provider configuration.
- `launchd` installation, secrets, private remote configuration, and encryption keys
  are not recovered from Git. Source: `00_System/v0.3_Recovery_Drill.md`.
- Scheduler jobs may discover/enrich/expire operational Candidates and rebuild
  derived state. They cannot approve Sources, Events, Impact Assertions, Analysis
  Runs, Forecasts, Recommendations, or Thesis confidence.
- Private hosted CI is metadata-only and asset-independent: it has read-only repository
  permission and receives no raw Source assets, Candidate DB, secrets, private identity,
  cache, or artifact upload. It cannot establish raw-byte recovery correctness.
- The strict local Gate remains required for integration and recovery acceptance.

### Forecast calibration and release time gates

- Ten Forecasts exist, but no natural Forecast resolution is yet available. The
  earliest real resolution date is 2026-10-31, so calibration is insufficient for a
  reliability claim.
- WP-530 is BLOCKED and future-date dependent; it cannot be completed by synthetic
  outcomes.
- WP-620 is BLOCKED and future-date dependent on the real 30-day Pilot and natural
  resolutions. Cadence records, reviewers, dates, and approvals cannot be backfilled.
- Engineering completion of WP-610/WP-611/WP-612 is separate from v0.3 release.
  WP-530, WP-620, and F-024 remain independent BLOCKED gates.
- The default release check remains v0.2 and reports the existing 18 v0.2 gates.
  The explicit --version 0.3 path implements F-023 as a read-only machine evaluation;
  it currently reports BLOCKED and cannot create the F-024 human packet, approval,
  tag, or release.

### Investment decision boundary

- Recommendations are research drafts. They are not buy, sell, position-size, or
  execution instructions.
- The system performs no automated investment action and has no broker, order, or
  portfolio execution integration.
- Technical strength, product release, adoption, and company benefit do not by
  themselves establish investment value. Valuation, pricing power, competition,
  expectations, and contradicting evidence require human judgment.

## Inferences

- Candidate volume materially above 10,000 rows, concurrent writers, or distributed
  readers may still make SQLite filtering or locking a bottleneck. The measured 10k
  single-user result does not settle 100k or concurrency behavior; future storage
  changes remain contingent on a measured miss at the applicable scale.
- Sparse Channel-to-entity links likely understate stale-company coverage. Filling
  those links may improve the freshness signal, but could also reveal more stale data
  rather than improving actual coverage.
- Model/provider variation is likely to change Analysis output quality and cost.
  Frozen run provenance makes comparison possible but does not remove model
  uncertainty or provider retention risk.
- A verified durable transaction reduces device-loss exposure only when ciphertext,
  its required receipt, and at least one matching private identity survive in separate
  custody. A second offline identity reduces single-key loss risk but increases the
  operator's key-custody surface.
- Missing or unpriced Discovery cost records may materially understate the known
  monthly total. The six-state monitor exposes the gap but cannot estimate it safely.

## Judgments

- Keep Markdown as authority, SQLite as the Candidate operational store, and the Web
  interface read-only until measured load or an approved multi-user requirement
  justifies a different architecture.
- Treat license metadata as a stop/go operational control, not a legal conclusion.
  Disable a Channel when status becomes restricted or uncertain and seek human review.
- Treat metadata-only CI as a fast tracked-metadata gate, never as a replacement for
  strict local validation, real Source asset verification, or a disposable restore.
- Maintain a verified durable backup at least every 24 hours. Treat missing, failed,
  invalid, or stale durable receipt state as P1 until explicit remote verification and
  a disposable restore succeed.
- Do not describe Forecast calibration as validated before real outcomes accumulate.
  Keep WP-530 and WP-620 open through their natural dates.
- Do not release v0.3 merely because engineering tests pass. Release requires the
  real Pilot, natural resolution evidence, human packet, and explicit decision.
- Preserve the no automated investment action boundary. Any future execution feature
  requires separate product, security, compliance, and human governance approval.
