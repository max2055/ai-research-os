## Inference

The recorded SSL exception is consistent with a transport interruption during the
two arXiv requests. That is an inference from the error string, not an independently
proven root cause. The retained records do not distinguish among a remote endpoint
termination, an intervening network or proxy condition, a local TLS-stack condition,
or another transient transport failure. The successful non-failing discovery Jobs
show that other scheduled work completed in the audited windows, but they do not
prove that either failed arXiv path was healthy.

## Operational judgment

Retain both failed Job Runs and their failed Candidate runs as immutable incident
evidence. Do not relabel them based on later success elsewhere. A transport change
and a new, separately identified run should be reviewed before considering the two
channels operationally recovered. This judgment changes no Source, Evidence,
Thesis, confidence, Forecast, Recommendation, or investment conclusion.
